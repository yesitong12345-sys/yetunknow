# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the site owner, who captures unfinished thoughts and ordinary moments privately, then deliberately curates selected material for public visitors. Public visitors arrive primarily through a desktop browser or a WeChat link and explore ideas, daily notes, and real project work.

## Product Purpose

“我的私人书桌” is a personal site that turns a conventional portfolio and journal into a tactile desk. Success means the owner can safely capture first, publish intentionally, withdraw safely, and let the public understand the person through traces of thinking and making rather than a conventional biography.

## Positioning

Private source notes and public posts are different objects: public work is a curated snapshot grown from a private original, never a visibility toggle on unfinished writing.

## Operating Context

The owner records from a phone, reviews and publishes deliberately, and treats the desktop home as a spatial index. Visitors browse three public collections: 奇思妙想、日常记录、项目作品. The studio is an owner-only working surface; the current build is explicitly a local demonstration until live Supabase authentication is connected.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Desktop is the complete primary experience; mobile changes layout but does not remove content, destinations, states, or feedback.
- Keyboard, pointer, and touch input must reach the same four primary destinations.
- Reduced motion replaces movement without removing state.
- Example content must be labelled and must not be presented as the owner’s real history.
- Formal hosting, domain, ICP, and production Supabase credentials remain deferred.

## Brand Commitments

The name is “我的私人书桌”. The binding visual asset is the supplied/generated top-down handmade desk image with real paper texture, a yellow note stack, open notebook, cobalt toolbox, plant, crayon, and locked wooden drawer. The visual character is tactile, childlike, restrained, and editorial; it must not regress to flat generic dashboard cards.

## Product Principles

- Capture privately before publishing publicly.
- Let physical traces reveal personality.
- Make desktop exploration memorable without hiding navigation.
- Preserve every important state across devices and accessibility preferences.
- Treat real personal material as evidence, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All primary objects require visible focus, Enter and Space activation, touch targets of at least 44px, semantic HTML labels, and reduced-motion equivalents.
